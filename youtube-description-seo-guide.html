YouTube Description SEO Guide

A YouTube description gives viewers more context about your video and can help organize useful information around your content. A good description should be natural, informative and relevant to the actual video.

1. Explain the Video Clearly

Start your description by explaining what the video is about. The first few lines are especially important because viewers may see them before expanding the full description.

Tell viewers what they can learn, discover or accomplish by watching the video.

2. Use Relevant Topic Phrases

Think about the words and phrases that accurately describe your video. Include them naturally instead of repeating the same keyword excessively.

If your video is about YouTube thumbnail design, relevant phrases might include "YouTube thumbnails," "thumbnail design," "better thumbnails" and "YouTube channel branding."

3. Add Useful Links

Your description can provide useful links for viewers. Depending on your content, these might include:

  • Your website
  • Related videos
  • Social media profiles
  • Products or services
  • Resources mentioned in the video
  • Affiliate links, when appropriate and properly disclosed

4. Use a Clear Structure

Long descriptions become easier to read when information is organized into sections. You can use short paragraphs, headings, bullet points and timestamps where appropriate.

5. Include a Simple Call to Action

Tell viewers what you would like them to do next. For example, you could invite them to subscribe, watch another related video, visit your website or leave a comment.

6. Keep Everything Accurate

Your description should match your video. Avoid adding unrelated keywords simply because they receive search traffic. Relevance is more useful than keyword stuffing.

Quick tip: Write the description for a real viewer first. Then review it and make sure the important topic phrases are included naturally.

Example Description Structure

A simple structure might look like this:

  1. One or two sentences explaining the video.
  2. A short list of what viewers will learn.
  3. Useful resources and links.
  4. Relevant timestamps if needed.
  5. A simple call to action.
  6. Relevant hashtags where appropriate.

Final Checklist

  • Does the description accurately explain the video?
  • Are important topic phrases included naturally?
  • Is the opening useful and clear?
  • Are links organized and relevant?
  • Is the call to action easy to understand?
  • Have unnecessary keyword repetitions been removed?

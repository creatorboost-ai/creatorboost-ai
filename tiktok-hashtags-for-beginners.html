TikTok Hashtags for Beginners

Hashtags can help organize your TikTok content around topics and make it easier for interested viewers to understand what your video is about. The goal is not to use as many hashtags as possible. The goal is to use relevant hashtags that match your content.

1. Start With Your Content Topic

Identify the main subject of your video before choosing hashtags. A cooking tutorial, fitness video, educational lesson and comedy clip will need very different hashtag sets.

2. Combine Broad and Specific Hashtags

A useful strategy is to combine broader topic hashtags with more specific ones.

  • Broad topic: #Fitness
  • Specific topic: #HomeWorkout
  • Audience-focused: #WorkoutForBeginners
  • Content-specific: #NoEquipmentWorkout

3. Think About Your Audience

Ask yourself what your target viewer might be interested in. A hashtag should make sense for both your content and the audience you want to reach.

4. Avoid Irrelevant Trending Hashtags

A popular hashtag is not automatically useful. If it has nothing to do with your video, adding it may make your content strategy less focused.

5. Create Hashtag Groups

Instead of creating a completely new hashtag strategy for every video, develop groups based on your content categories.

For example, an educational creator could have one group for YouTube tutorials, another for social media marketing and another for creator productivity.

6. Review Your Results

Experiment with relevant hashtag combinations and review your content performance over time. Look for patterns in which topics and formats attract the most useful audience.

Quick tip: Relevance should come before volume. Five carefully selected hashtags can be more useful for your content strategy than a long list of unrelated tags.

Simple TikTok Hashtag Formula

A beginner-friendly approach is:

  • 1 to 2 broad topic hashtags
  • 2 to 3 specific topic hashtags
  • 1 audience or niche hashtag
  • Optional branded hashtag

Final Checklist

  • Does every hashtag relate to the video?
  • Does the hashtag describe the topic or audience?
  • Are you avoiding irrelevant trends?
  • Are you testing different relevant combinations?
  • Are your hashtags supporting a clear content strategy?

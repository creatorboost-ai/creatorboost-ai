Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CreatorBoost AI and video metadata.

Is CreatorBoost AI free?

Yes. The basic browser-based CreatorBoost AI generator is free and does not require a user account.

Does a good title guarantee more views?

No. A good title can improve clarity and potentially help discoverability, but views also depend on content quality, audience fit, thumbnails, retention and platform recommendations.

Can I use CreatorBoost AI for YouTube Shorts?

Yes. CreatorBoost AI can generate content ideas, titles, descriptions, tags and hashtags that can be adapted for YouTube Shorts.

Does CreatorBoost AI guarantee YouTube growth?

No. CreatorBoost AI provides content-planning suggestions and cannot guarantee views, subscribers, rankings, monetization or income.

Should I use every generated tag?

No. Select only keywords that genuinely describe your video. Avoid misleading or repetitive keyword stuffing.
